\pageheading{Edge treatment, ideal stability and linear response}
The axisymmetric benchmark uses the $s_{\rm pol}=0.995$ cut. For the linear $n=3$
response the edge treatment matters more than any equilibrium difference above. DCON
without a wall finds every hard cut $n=3$ ideal-unstable; the full LCFS is stable.
The drive is the finite edge current density at the cut facing vacuum: DCON uses only
$p'$, and the pressure-offset cut reproduces GPEC to $10^{-8}$. A linear response of an
unstable plasma is not a benchmark, so the linear lane uses the full LCFS (psihigh
0.999999, no wall), which is also what the collaborators' MARS-F and MARS-K runs solved.
Lanes that need a cut (free boundary, VMEC-type codes, nonlinear 3D) use the FB-taper
family: modx03 $p'$ and $FF'$ times a smooth taper reaching zero with zero slope at

\subsection*{RMP coil set}
GPEC \texttt{iter\_bl2\{u,m,l\}.dat} is canonical: $I_k=90\,{\rm kA}\cos(3\phi_c+\phi_{\rm row})$
with $\phi_c$ each coil's centroid and $\phi_{\rm row}=(0,0,0)$ or $(0,\pi,0)$, $n=+3$
(MARS \texttt{RNTOR=-3} is the conjugate). The middle row sits $-3.3^\circ$ off the
others; $\pm3.3^\circ$ changes the $(0,0,0)$ resonant flux by about $\mp27$\,\%.
Xingting's coil polylines, used in MARS-F and NTVTOK, give 1.6 times the bl2 resonant
flux for $(0,0,0)$ and 1.1 times for $(0,\pi,0)$, so up to $1.7\times$ of the historical
GPEC/MARS gap is coil geometry. The KIN6D L3 Biot--Savart vacuum $b_{mn}$ matches the
GPEC real-space spectrum to about four digits; the MARS-F bl2 sheet-coil model matches
L3 to 1--2\,\% after row factors 1.070/1.022/0.970 (U/M/L).

\subsection*{Nonlinear 3D against linear response}
A finite-amplitude 3D equilibrium is not assumed to equal the linear response. The
comparison scales the RMP current down until the response per unit current converges.
Nested GVEC equilibria exist only up to a few per cent of 90 kAt (vacuum edge
displacement about 0.18 m at full current for $(0,\pi,0)$). On the 0.995 cut with a
fixed boundary displaced by the coils' vacuum normal field (ntor 1, tolerance $10^{-9}$),
the $n=3$ response per unit amplitude is constant to 1\,\% $(0,0,0)$ and 5\,\%
$(0,\pi,0)$ between 1 and 10\,\% of 90 kAt, at the radial discretisation floor
(ne32 against ne48: 3--9\,\%). Against GPEC with the same boundary condition
(\texttt{fixed\_boundary\_flag}) the harmonics on $\psi_N\le0.8$ agree to 3--7\,\%
($\delta B\cdot n$) and 4--14\,\% ($\xi\cdot n$) for $(0,0,0)$ and 7--11/12--16\,\% for
$(0,\pi,0)$, with amplitude ratio 1.0 and phase offset about $1^\circ$; the $\xi$ jump
across $q=3$ agrees to a ratio 0.7--1.4. This is the first nonlinear-3D against
linear agreement in the small-amplitude limit. Free-boundary GPEC is not comparable
here: its edge response is 2--4 times vacuum on this $n=3$-unstable hard cut.
